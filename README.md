# HealthConnect Rwanda: Healthcare Analytics & Business Intelligence Project

## Project Overview

HealthConnect Rwanda is an end-to-end healthcare analytics project designed to transform healthcare operational, clinical, financial and patient experience data into actionable business insights.

Using SQL Server for data analysis and Power BI for data visualisation, this project explores patient demographics, appointment activity, clinical services, financial performance, operational capacity, insurance claims and patient satisfaction metrics. The project also includes comprehensive data quality assessment and KPI validation between SQL outputs and Power BI dashboards.

The objective was to demonstrate how a healthcare organisation can leverage data analytics to support evidence-based decision-making, improve operational efficiency and strengthen financial oversight.

---

## Business Problem

Healthcare organisations generate large volumes of data across multiple systems, including patient records, appointments, diagnoses, billing, insurance claims and patient feedback.

Without proper analysis and validation, decision-makers may face:

- Inconsistent reporting across platforms
- Poor visibility into operational performance
- Data quality issues that affect decision-making
- Inefficient claims management processes
- Limited understanding of patient experience trends
- Financial reporting inaccuracies

This project addresses these challenges through healthcare analytics, dashboard reporting and business-focused recommendations.

## Key Insights

### 1. Patient Demographics

- Analysed 20,000 patient records.
- Male patients: 10,090 (50.45%).
- Female patients: 9,910 (49.55%).
- Patient records were distributed relatively evenly across Rwanda's provinces.

**Business Insight:** Healthcare demand appears geographically balanced rather than concentrated in a single region.

### 2. Service Demand Analysis

A total of 5,000 appointment records were analysed.

Appointment types were distributed almost equally:

- Follow-up: 1,694 (33.88%)
- Consultation: 1,656 (33.12%)
- Emergency: 1,650 (33.00%)

**Business Insight:** Demand is spread across all three service categories, highlighting the need for balanced resource planning.

### 3. Clinical Activity Analysis

Most frequently recorded diagnoses:

1. Gastritis
2. Diabetes
3. Hypertension
4. Malaria
5. Upper Respiratory Infection

Most requested laboratory tests:

- Malaria
- CBC
- Glucose
- Urinalysis

Most prescribed medications:

- Amlodipine
- Omeprazole
- Metformin
- Amoxicillin
- Artemether-Lumefantrine

**Business Insight:** Chronic conditions such as diabetes and hypertension represent a significant portion of healthcare activity and may support preventative healthcare planning.

### 4. Financial Performance

Key findings included:

- Highest monthly revenue recorded in September.
- Lowest monthly revenue recorded in December.
- Significant fluctuations in monthly revenue performance.
- Insurance claims evenly split between Pending and Approved statuses.

**Business Insight:** Revenue variability and claim-processing delays highlight opportunities to improve financial management and reimbursement tracking.

### 5. Patient Experience

Key metric:

- Average patient satisfaction score: 2.99 / 5.

**Business Insight:** Patient experience requires further investigation to identify service quality drivers and opportunities for improvement.

## Data Quality Assessment

A dedicated data validation and cleansing process was conducted before analysis.

### Data Type Issues

Identified:

- Phone numbers stored as numeric values instead of text.
- Revenue month field stored as text rather than date format.

### Consistency Issues

Examples included:

- Child records associated with adult occupations.
- Implausible height and weight values for young patients.

### Relationship Issues

Unmatched records were identified between:

- Appointments and admissions.
- Diagnoses and clinical transactions.
- Billing and payments.
- Feedback and clinical activity records.

### Validation Outcomes

SQL calculations were successfully reconciled against Power BI KPI outputs for:

- Total patients
- Total appointments
- Total revenue
- Total doctors
- Average patient satisfaction

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Data Governance

- Standardise identifiers across datasets.
- Implement validation rules.
- Establish ongoing data quality monitoring.

### 2. Strengthen Financial Reconciliation

- Reconcile billing, payment and claim records.
- Improve accuracy of financial reporting.

### 3. Optimise Claims Management

- Track ageing claims.
- Improve follow-up processes.
- Monitor pending claim volumes.

### 4. Standardise KPI Definitions

- Develop a KPI dictionary.
- Align SQL and Power BI business logic.

### 5. Enhance Resource Planning

- Use validated appointment trends for workforce planning.
- Align capacity with service demand.

### 6. Improve Patient Experience Monitoring

- Investigate drivers of satisfaction ratings.
- Analyse feedback by location and service type.
